How it works

  1. Choose a plan Use Find my plan or browse all plans.
  2. Create an account Sign up with your details.
  3. Confirm your order Review the plan and you will get a membership ID such as FI-123456.
  4. Receive your SIM Your SIM (and phone, if included) arrives in 3–5 working days.

Questions

What is the difference between Fone, Gap and Flipper?

All three are run by Friendly Internet. Fone is the classic network, Gap has unlimited data options and Flipper has the lowest prices.

How long is the contract?

All plans are on a 2-year contract.

What happens if I am under 18?

A £10 per month spending cap is applied and the account is marked as age-restricted.

Can I get a phone with my plan?

Yes. "All-in" plans include a handset and there are "Just phone" options too. Available models: iPhone 14 Pro, iPhone 14, iPhone 13, Samsung S22 and Samsung S21.

How do I see my plans after signing up?

Open My account. If you are logged in your memberships are shown straight away.
